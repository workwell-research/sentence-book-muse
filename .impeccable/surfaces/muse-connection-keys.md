OWN-WORLD: Inherit Connect’s plain warm-paper sections, fine dividers, serif headings, graphite text, native labeled inputs and quiet actions. No new identity or assets.
STORY: Copy public instructions, choose permissions, issue a key, copy it directly into Muse, then dismiss its one-time display. List and revoke by name and expiry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing inbox and shared-summary remain above the new connection section; the new section explains the next action before its form. Small screens stack controls with 44px targets.
FORM: Narrow extension of an incumbent operational surface; no concept roll or generated comp applies. Expiry, failures, one-time display and revocation are visible states. No decorative motion is introduced.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
