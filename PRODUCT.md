# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite + React + TypeScript Web prototype, Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions. Latest user request explicitly targets Web handwriting; earlier Flutter discussion was exploratory. Existing preferences use localStorage; handwriting demo input is session-only.

## Users and purpose
People who want to practise English about their own lives, ideas and work. A requested page becomes part of a growing theme binder after the learner reviews it.

## Current prototype
The root route demonstrates real handwriting, pen motion, marker/annotation, a personal English line and independent vector PDF export. It uses labeled synthetic sample content. The earlier interest/topic workflow is preserved at /setup. No AI call or learning result is implied by completing the animation.

## Workflow
First choose interests (multiple suggestions or custom text, editable and skippable). Then optionally write a daily note or a short post in the learner’s language, choose A2/B1/B2, generate with Muse, review content and filing destination, explicitly approve, and practise Copy. Interest onboarding and local note saving work; generation, filing and practice remain planned.

## Constraints
New first-party implementation. The prior app remains independent. No copied source, private lessons, illustrations or audio. No account, sync, MCP, full five-pass course or saved speech assets. Interests guide the content Muse prioritizes; they do not create or constrain binder categories. Ordinary short posts can seed useful communication practice without judging their author. No SNS account connection or automatic collection is included. The shelf starts empty, with no fixed theme names or theme count. New binders grow from the learner’s pages and require explicit approval. No bundled curriculum. A labeled, newly authored synthetic sentence is bundled only for the handwriting demonstration.

## Brand commitments
The Sentence Book — Learn digitally. Keep it beautifully. The new identity is a cream ruled notebook with real handwriting strokes, pale markers and small annotations. The owner explicitly retired the typewriter motif and its key/case animations. Korean Studygram × restrained editorial design; the note itself is the artifact. Learning should progressively produce a beautiful page worth printing. No rankings, confetti or chat interface.

## Evidence and open inputs
A user-supplied rebuild design exists. The owner clarified that standalone lesson-schema-v2 is unavailable and five themes are unnecessary. The new contract is documented in docs/generation-contract.md; actual Meta model access is outstanding. Abu Dhabi presentation preparation is a proposed demo topic; no personal speech, claims or specialist content has been supplied.

## Accessibility
Readable labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, native form controls, reduced motion and a usable narrow layout.
