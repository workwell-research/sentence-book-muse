# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vite + React + TypeScript, Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions; localStorage. Explicitly selected by the user.

## Users and purpose
People who want to practise English about their own lives, ideas and work. A requested page becomes part of a growing theme binder after the learner reviews it.

## Workflow
Write a topic in the learner's language, choose A2/B1/B2, generate with Muse, review content and filing destination, explicitly approve, and practise Copy. These are planned capabilities; the scaffold only saves a topic note.

## Constraints
New first-party implementation. The prior app remains independent. No copied source, private lessons, illustrations or audio. No account, sync, MCP, full five-pass course or saved speech assets. Five initial theme names are classification vocabulary, with zero bundled lessons. New binders require explicit approval.

## Brand commitments
The Sentence Book; a quiet study, warm paper, sage tones, books and a simple typewriter. The writing experience is one line at a time. No rankings, confetti or chat interface.

## Evidence and open inputs
A user-supplied rebuild design exists. Standalone lesson-schema-v2 and actual Meta model access are outstanding. Abu Dhabi presentation preparation is a proposed demo topic; no personal speech, claims or specialist content has been supplied.

## Accessibility
Readable labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, native form controls, reduced motion and a usable narrow layout.
