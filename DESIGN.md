# Design

Preserve the user-pinned warm study and paper world; implement new source and geometry. No assets from the prior prototype.

- Desk #f5f1e8, paper #fffdf7, ink #363a32, muted ink #676b60, sage #4d604b.
- Lora is installed from Fontsource and self-hosted. Japanese uses the platform's readable Mincho face for headings and system sans for controls.
- The initial wooden shelf is empty. A short invitation explains that themes grow from pages; no placeholder binders or fixed theme count. When filing exists, show only approved binders with readable labels and page counts.
- The desktop puts the shelf beside a paper note. Narrow viewports stack the two; the header provides a direct link to the note.
- Focus rings, selection and the caret use intentional colors. Form feedback describes actual saving; unavailable generation is explicitly disabled.
- No generated illustration or fake lesson is needed for this scaffold.
